( The boring-but-honest page )

Cookies.

No tracking cookies · No ad pixels · Nothing follows you

Short version: this site doesn't use cookies to track you. No advertising pixels, no fingerprinting, no third-party trackers watching you scroll. The characters watch you — but that stays strictly between you and them, on your screen.

What we actually store

To remember your choices between visits, the site keeps a few small values in your browser's localStorage. They are functional only, never leave your device, and identify no one:

KeyWhat it remembers
cs-mascot-variantWhich driver you picked, so your world is waiting for you next time.
cs-theme / cs-theme-versionThe color world that driver painted.
cs-soundWhether you left the sound on or off.
cs-game-best / cs-game-initialsYour best DOT SHOT score and the initials you used.

One honest note about the game: if you choose to save a score, those initials and that score are sent to our server so they can appear on the public leaderboard. That's the whole trip they take.

Analytics

We don't use Google Analytics or any cookie-based analytics. If we measure traffic, we do it with a self-hosted, cookie-free counter that stores nothing on your device and identifies no one — it tells us "someone visited", not "who".

Third parties

None. Fonts are self-hosted, the code is ours, and nothing on this page phones home to anyone else.

Clearing it

Everything above lives in your browser. Clear your browsing data for feyron.site and it's gone — the site simply forgets your driver, your theme and your high score. The characters will pretend not to be hurt.

Questions

Write us at hello@feyron.site and a human (yes, a human) will answer.
