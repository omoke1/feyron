( Overview & Architecture )

Bridging stablecoin liquidity and local payment rails in one unified experience.

Businesses and professionals operating across borders face slow international wires, fragmented checkout tools, and manual reconciliation between crypto/stablecoin treasuries and local bank accounts.

With Qreva, Feyron built a unified payment experience that combines stablecoin settlement (USDC/USDT) with instant local bank transfers, QR scan-to-pay, bill payments, and SoftPOS card acceptance — all reconciled automatically in under 60 seconds.

01 · Stablecoin Settlement

On-chain speed with instant local payout

Accept and settle USDC and USDT alongside local currency rails through licensed liquidity partners, with automatic ledger matching.

02 · Multi-Rail Super App

Transfers, SoftPOS & Scan-to-Pay

Turns any smartphone into a point-of-sale terminal while supporting instant bank transfers, utility payments, and agent commissions.

03 · Automated Reconciliation

Zero manual spreadsheet matching

Every inbound stablecoin or bank transaction triggers real-time webhooks, instant receipts, and clean accounting entries.
