( Overview & Architecture )

Idle treasury should never sit stranded on the wrong chain while positions face risk.

Managing liquidity across multiple chains usually requires someone watching dashboards around the clock to bridge funds, rebalance yield, or top up collateral when markets move.

Sweepify uses a coordinated three-agent system built around Circle USDC and Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol (CCTP) to keep treasury capital productive, move native USDC across chains without wrapped-token risk, and protect active positions before liquidation thresholds are reached.

01 · Scout Agent

Continuous cross-chain yield & risk telemetry

Monitors treasury balances, lending rates, and collateral health factors across supported networks in real time.

02 · Router Agent

Native USDC bridging via Circle CCTP

Executes burn-and-mint native USDC transfers across chains deterministically whenever a higher-yield or safety rebalance is triggered.

03 · Guard Agent

Automated pre-liquidation collateral top-ups

Detects drawdown risk early and routes reserve USDC to top up margin automatically within pre-approved treasury limits.
