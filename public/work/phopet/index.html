( The booking )

Pets can't sit still.
Models can.

→

Everyone photographs their pet constantly — and still never owns a single portrait worth framing. Real studio sessions are expensive, and the client chews the props. Phopet is our answer, built in-house: the only AI studio built exclusively for pets.

Upload a handful of phone photos and Phopet trains a personal model of your animal. From then on it can shoot your pet on demand: forty-plus themed albums, illustrated stories, print-grade enlargements. The camera roll goes in, the gallery wall comes out.

From camera roll to gallery wall in one treat.

Shot 03the wardrobe

Forty albums deep.

Chill, Travel, Studio, Adventure, Jobs, Halloween — every album is a full styled session with its own art direction, and the rail keeps going past forty. A cat in a spa towel sits next to a firefighter in the flames. The range is the product: one pet, every character it could ever play.

Roll 03 · Fr 08
Album · Superheroes
Shot 04the plot twist

Your pet, the hero.

Stories push past portraits: pick a tale and Phopet casts your pet as the lead — a shiba in a Halloween ghost story, another one carving down a ski slope in goggles. Each story is a generated sequence with covers, chapters and a title card, ready to share.

Print · A2
Shot 05the print

Made to be framed.

An upscaling pass takes any generation to print resolution — the section shows the point of it all: a ghost-costume portrait, framed, on a real wall over the sideboard. Beyond that, the images flow onto hoodies, phone cases and very serious reindeer socks. Digital memories, physical objects.

The welcome pack is the whole pitch in one number: train your pet's profile and download fifty photos for 9.90€. One payment, no subscription, a sunburst sticker pointing at forty more albums. Credits only exist so you can come back for Halloween.

Album · Summer

( Under the hood )

The studio behind the studio.

01 · Pipeline

A model per pet

Each pet gets its own fine-tuned model, trained from a handful of phone photos. Identity survives across every album style — same ears, same spots, new wardrobe. 10K+ images shipped and counting.

02 · Product

A studio, not a filter

Albums, stories and print-grade upscaling hang off one gallery-first UI, localized in English, Spanish and Japanese. Queues and generation status stay visible — training a model feels like waiting for film, not for a spinner.

03 · Commerce

Priced like a treat

A 9.90€ welcome pack, one-tap checkout and refillable credits — no subscription anywhere. Low friction in, honest pricing throughout, built to convert pet-lovers who've never touched an AI tool.
