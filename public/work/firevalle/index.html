( Overview & Architecture )

Accepting digital and stablecoin payments in seconds — without custom checkout code.

Merchants, agencies, and digital businesses needed a frictionless way to invoice clients and collect USDC.e and digital token payments without building custom smart-contract checkouts from scratch.

FlowPay lets any business generate shareable payment links for products or invoices in seconds, accept instant digital payments on desktop or mobile, and track revenue analytics in real time.

01 · Shareable Payment Links

Create a checkout link in 10 seconds

Generate branded payment links for services, subscriptions, or one-off invoices and share them via WhatsApp, email, or social channels.

02 · Instant Settlement

Direct-to-treasury USDC.e & token checkout

Customers pay in one click with mobile-friendly wallet integration and instant on-chain confirmation.

03 · Real-Time Revenue Dashboard

Live conversion & payout analytics

Monitor paid links, customer transaction history, and daily revenue performance from a clean merchant command center.
